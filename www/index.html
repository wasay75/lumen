Add to your device

  1. Tap Share in the Safari toolbar.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Lumen from your home screen — it runs full screen and works offline.
  1. Open the browser menu.
  2. Choose Install Lumen — in Safari on a Mac it is Add to Dock.
  3. Lumen gets its own window and icon, and works offline.

How to play

  1. Tap a strand to turn it a quarter turn.
  2. Join every end. No strand may point at a wall or at nothing.
  3. Light flows from the source outward. The puzzle opens when the whole weave glows.

On a mouse, right-click to turn a strand the other way.
